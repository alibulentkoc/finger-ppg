Tap a camera button to begin. The preview should look evenly red when your finger covers the lens.

Front camera (recommended on iPhone)

  1. Turn screen brightness all the way up.
  2. Tap "Front camera + screen light". The camera opens right away. Tap Allow if asked. The top of the page turns white.
  3. Rest your fingertip over the front camera, top center of the screen. Touch lightly. Pressing hard squeezes out the blood and flattens the signal.
  4. Keep your hand and phone still. Sit, rest the forearm on a table.
  5. Wait about 10 s for heart rate. HRV values appear after about 20 clean beats. Use 1 to 2 minutes for a stable RMSSD.

Rear camera

Tap "Rear camera" to open it. Cover the main lens (the top-left lens on most iPhones). Safari usually cannot switch on the flash from a web page, so use a bright lamp or daylight behind your finger. If the browser supports it, the page turns the flash on for you.

Try biofeedback

Breathe in for 5 s and out for 5 s. After a minute, the lower chart should show a smooth wave, one cycle per breath.
