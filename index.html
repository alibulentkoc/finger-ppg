How it works

Take a reading

  1. Sit down, rest your forearm on a table, and breathe normally for a minute.
  2. Turn screen brightness all the way up.
  3. Pick a session: 2, 3, or 5 min for a resting reading, or 10 min breathe for the breathing exercise.
  4. Tap "Start: front camera" and allow camera access. The top of the screen turns white to light your finger.
  5. Rest your fingertip over the front camera, top center of the screen. Touch lightly. Pressing hard squeezes out the blood and flattens the signal.
  6. Hold still. The timer pauses if you lift your finger. Your report opens when the session ends. Tap Stop to end early.

Rear camera

Cover the main lens (top-left on most iPhones). Safari usually cannot turn on the flash from a web page, so use a bright lamp or daylight behind your finger. If the browser allows it, the flash turns on by itself.

Breathing exercise

Breathe in as the circle grows and out as it shrinks. The out-breath is a little longer, which is calming. Breathe gently. Slow does not mean deep.

The circle turns from amber to blue to green as your heart rate starts to rise and fall smoothly with your breath. This is called coherence. The bar under the circle shows how much of the session you spent in high, medium, and low coherence. The coaching line tells you to slow down or speed up if your breathing drifts from the guide. Most people reach high coherence at 5 to 7 breaths per minute.

What the numbers mean

Heart rate (beats/min)
Beats per minute, from the median of your last few beats.
HRV, heart rate variability (RMSSD, ms)
The small, natural change in time from one beat to the next. RMSSD is the root mean square of successive differences between beat intervals. It mainly reflects the calming (parasympathetic) side of your nervous system. It is usually higher when you are rested and lower after poor sleep, stress, illness, alcohol, or hard exercise. Many healthy adults measure about 20 to 80 ms at rest, and values drop with age.
SDNN (ms)
Standard deviation of normal-to-normal beat intervals. Overall variability across the whole session.
pNN50 (%)
Percent of successive beat intervals that differ by more than 50 ms.
Clean beats
Beats kept after removing missed, extra, and mistimed beats caused by movement or pressure changes.
Coherence (%)
The share of your heart rate variation that sits in one smooth wave, usually your breathing rhythm. High is 70% or more, medium is 45 to 70%, low is below 45%. It is calculated from the last 64 s of clean beats.
Quality (High, Medium, Low)
Based on how many beats passed the checks. Treat Low-quality HRV values as rough estimates.

Getting comparable readings

HRV changes through the day. Measure at the same time each day, ideally in the morning, in the same position. After 3 good resting readings, the report compares each new one with your usual value. Breathing sessions are kept separate, because slow breathing raises HRV.

Privacy

Everything runs on your phone. No video or data leaves the device. Past results are saved only in this browser, and you can clear them from the report.

Limits

This tool uses contact photoplethysmography (PPG): the camera sees tiny brightness changes in your fingertip as blood pulses through it. It is for learning and general wellness. It is not a medical device and cannot diagnose any condition. If you have symptoms such as chest pain, fainting, or a racing or irregular heartbeat, contact a clinician.
